Chapter One

About me

An aspiring Marketing Growth Analyst with a deep curiosity for why people make the choices they make.

I studied psychology at Bryn Mawr College and now explore data and human behavior through UC Berkeley's Master's in Computational Social Science.

My work sits at the intersection of data and human behavior — shaped by experience as a marketing and growth analyst across B2B and consumer tech startups. I've worked on CRM management, paid media campaigns, and marketing analytics, as well as consumer research and insights in a CPG environment. I enjoy translating messy data into clear decisions, whether that's optimizing a campaign or uncovering what actually drives user behavior.

Off the clock, I'm an obsessive home baker. My specialty is shokupan (Japanese milk bread) and sesame bagels — both demand the same precision and patience I bring to every analysis.
