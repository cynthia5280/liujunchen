03 · Email
Email channel

One message wasn't working for users at different awareness stages.

The problem

  • One email blast, no segmentation
  • Low open and click-through rates
  • Same message reaching every user, regardless of engagement

Hypothesis

  • Users sit at different stages of awareness
  • One message can't serve all of them
  • Behavior-based segmentation should drive relevance
Segmentation — based on weekly email open rate

Active — Segment A

Opened and clicked emails ≥ 2× in the past week. Higher engagement, ready for feature-forward messaging.

Inactive — Segment B

Opened or clicked fewer than 2×, or not at all. Lower familiarity — needs re-engagement and brand introduction.

What this told us

Over-narrow targeting capped reach and burned spend on repeat impressions — frequency hit 6.68 with zero downloads. The broader test reached 2.3× more users at half the CPM and produced actual downloads. The hypothesis for the next test: broader, interest-driven audiences will outperform demographic stacking.

Result

Audience D — Self-Love delivered the highest reach, lowest CPC, and best CTR. Users with psychology and self-love interests resonated most with Joopi's "real connection" positioning. The decision: reallocate budget to D, and test adjacent wellness and identity-aligned interest segments next.

07 · Takeaways
What we learned

Two channels, two jobs in the funnel.

Email and paid ads validated as complementary — each owns a different stage of the user journey.

Channel role
Top of funnel
Meta Ads
Brand awareness & new user acquisition
Bottom of funnel
Email
Activation & re-engagement of existing users
Headline finding

The two channels are not interchangeable — they cover different jobs. Meta drives awareness through interest targeting; email converts and reactivates users who already know Joopi. Together, they form a full-funnel acquisition system.

Where to focus next
  1. Double down on Self-Love. Reallocate paid budget to D and adjacent psychology / wellness interest segments.
  2. Deepen email segmentation. Extend behavior-based segments beyond Active vs Inactive to lifecycle and intent signals.
